# ADR 0008 — versioned Admin recovery period

Status: DOCUMENTED DESIGN / ASSUMPTION details; explicit owner control confirmed by UD-001. Traces: [owner direction](../source/2026-10-15-owner-decisions.md), PRD FR-117–120/BR-048–052, decision record OQ-006/020. This supersedes an unconditional 30×24h calculation in derived contracts, not the original source bytes or legal policy.

Decision: scoped MFA Admin publishes an immutable RETENTION policy containing positive integer `recovery_days`, IANA `recovery_timezone`, calendar-day algorithm and legal-envelope evidence. Initial proposed policy is 30 days/Asia/Hebron, based on the PRD's calendar-day wording. Duration value is configurable; no permanent 30-day/720-hour CHECK. Policy edits require reason, idempotency, If-Match, audit/outbox and independent legal bounds; normal Admin cannot waive those bounds or alter a deletion already confirmed.

Deletion confirmation transaction locks canonical account, reads effective policy, stores policy ID/version, recovery_days, recovery_timezone, cutoff algorithm, confirmed_at UTC and computed restore_until UTC; immediately disables ordinary access/freezes wallet. Compute the same wall clock recovery_days later in captured zone; on a nonexistent target select next valid instant, on an ambiguous fold select later instant (engineering assumptions, not invented owner answers). Persist computed UTC deadline once, so later policy/timezone-database updates do not retroactively move it. Original timezone-policy version remains evidence; a new Admin policy affects future confirmations only.

Restore requires independently verified account ownership, trusted server receipt strictly `< restore_until`, and request PENDING before purge claim. Equality is expired. Serialize restore/purge on the same user/request rows; durable pre-cutoff receipt must win its defined lock arbitration before irreversible purge starts. An old client timestamp, general Admin session, or current policy value cannot authorize restore. Restore creates no signup grant, preserves submitted snapshots and uncanceled wallet. Final purge/cancellation requires proved resolution of holds and one DELETION_CANCEL witness; no blind timeout refund or indefinite lawful-retention exemption.

The legal_envelope_ref must resolve to an immutable independently approved LEGAL_RETENTION_ENVELOPE policy record with permitted duration bounds, approved jurisdiction/zone and evidence; its approval scope is separate from ordinary retention-policy editing and is not exposed by the retention write endpoint. Policy-kind/reference guards reject fabricated or revoked envelopes. No legal minimum/maximum or jurisdiction is invented. Reject a policy outside independently approved legal envelope; if unavailable, real-user deletion/retention activation remains G-LEGAL even while design can be completed/tested with synthetic policy fixtures. Dedicated emergency/legal changes to existing requests are not a silent settings side effect and remain an explicitly unprovided workflow.

Verification obligations: Admin changes policy after A's deletion but before B's; A retains old deadline/snapshot, B captures new one. Test invalid/zero/negative days, no MFA/scope, stale If-Match, missing legal envelope, DST gap/fold, exact cutoff, simultaneous restore/purge, unchanged balance on restore and cancellation once after final purge. Runtime tests NOT RUN in early documentation phases.
