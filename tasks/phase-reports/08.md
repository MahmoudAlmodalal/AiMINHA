Profile nationality/residency, desired degree, major, completed qualification,
GPA+declared scale, languages+CEFR level and preferred countries retain null as
Unknown. A confirmed empty list is an explicit declaration of no items; it is
not an implicit default. Supplied but unconfirmed values cannot satisfy rules.
Self-confirmation is not independent credential verification. Country/language
code syntax is bounded, not an authoritative nationality/credential dictionary.

Catalog prose and search facets never become machine rules automatically. A
current MODERATION Admin with server MFA reviews a separate immutable rule set
bound to the exact published catalog version/hash, source and criterion indices.
A complete basis must account for every criterion; source freshness must be
CURRENT for approval. A missing/empty/incomplete basis cannot imply eligibility.
Unverified requirements and unknown/stale source evidence yield unknown checks.
Flat mandatory rules are conjunctive; unsupported OR/exclusion/credential rules
must stay unsupported rather than approximated into false disqualification.

Known mandatory failure always yields INELIGIBLE, even with high fit or an
incomplete basis. Otherwise unresolved mandatory evidence, absent/incomplete
basis or all-unknown comparisons yield INSUFFICIENT_INFORMATION. ELIGIBLE needs
a complete reviewed basis, at least one known comparison and all mandatory rules
satisfied. No AI/provider call or API result/status override exists.

Coverage is the sum of fixed weights for domains with at least one comparison
and all comparisons known. Known failure counts as evidence with zero fit;
unknown domains remain null. Domain fit is the mean of binary matches; total fit
is the weighted mean over covered domains, rounded half-up to basis points, only
when coverage>=7000. These formula details and the preferred-country interest
proxy are explicit assumptions, not inferred calibration. Confidence is
UNCALIBRATED. Fit is never an admission probability and cannot change hard status.

Within-scale normalization is finite value/maximum for PERCENT_100, GPA_4_0 and
GPA_5_0. Different known scales and UNKNOWN scales produce unknown GPA checks;
no institutional equivalence is asserted. Language minimums use explicitly
stated CEFR levels for the same language; no proof authentication or inferred
IELTS/TOEFL conversion is implemented.

Private actions recheck active/verified/adult ownership and session proof under
the account lock. Head changes use CAS/If-Match and idempotency. Evaluations pin
profile/catalog/rule/policy/engine/weight versions and time. Reads and receipt
replays revalidate those current heads, source permission and freshness. Generic
receipts retain opaque references only; private facts/evidence stay in their
owned snapshots. Audit contains metadata only and mutations roll back on failure.
PostgreSQL protects immutable evidence, owner FKs, complete check identity and
rule-first aggregate status. Comparisons and fit arithmetic trust the application
engine; no full second SQL engine or production role/RLS certification is claimed.
