## Delivered behavior and bounded coverage

- Owned saved scholarships and per-reviewed-round application records, with real
  pagination and all-owned personal state counts. Lifetime owner/scholarship/round
  uniqueness prevents replacing an older round or terminal record silently.
- PREPARING→READY→SUBMITTED→UNDER_REVIEW→ACCEPTED/REJECTED, plus WITHDRAWN and the
  documented ordinary back/direct-outcome edges. Commands require current student
  session, verified adult ownership, CSRF, stable idempotency and revision CAS.
  No extension, AI or platform action submits to an external portal.
- Contiguous immutable status/activity/correction history with prior/to state,
  source, actor, observed time, server record time, notes, source/profile versions
  and selected-reference/hash manifests. Evidence-only correction appends; ordinary
  terminal reversal and implicit state/source overwrite are refused.
- Exact reviewed document requirements and owned approved document-version or
  accepted/scanned upload selections. Empty checklist remains unknown and cannot
  become Ready. Deleted/unavailable draft selections project as missing, retain
  their references in history, and cannot pass Ready/submission revalidation.
- Submission requires explicit owner confirmation after external submission. Exact
  artifact references and hashes are frozen at the submission event. Master edits
  and restore append document versions without mutating submitted evidence. SQL
  binds real tracker freezes to submitted artifacts in both directions; a Preparing
  tracker cannot fabricate a submitted snapshot. Ordinary uploaded-byte deletion
  is protected, including direct state changes outside the legal workflow.
- An explicit Preparing-only same-round source rebase pins newly reviewed source/
  profile/checklist evidence and clears draft selections after confirmation. Older
  requirements, events and manifests remain immutable. Submitted/terminal source
  rebases and arbitrary round reassignment are refused.
- Separate sourced/dated official-status evidence does not change student-reported
  state. Real official ingestion remains503 pending a lawful documented feed.
  Fixed LOCAL synthetic reports say SYNTHETIC_LOCAL and `verified_official=false`;
  a student cannot submit authority/origin flags or claim official verification.
- Owner-scoped submitted-artifact deletion review requests are separate immutable
  PENDING_LEGAL_REVIEW records. They do not mark content erased or thaw evidence.
  Canonical approved expired-account purge planning can mark protected exact
  uploads for removal; ordinary deletion cannot impersonate this approval. Actual
  physical/body erasure remains externally gated and is not falsely acknowledged.
- Current approved published fresh CONFIRMED deadline revisions and four reminder
  thresholds, with exact sponsor-zone evidence and UTC instants. Unknown, rolling,
  date-only, ambiguous, stale, superseded or revoked dates create no live countdown
  or timed schedule. Current display never trusts a historical application snapshot
  as the current deadline. Delivery rechecks recipient, source/head, relationship,
  optional preferences and remaining deadline before one private inbox effect.
- Per-recipient/event-version/offset dedupe with one scheduled/delivered logical
  attempt. Cancelled attempts retain history; opting back in before due can create
  another attempt without duplicate delivery. Finite durable worker batches/cursors
  recover retries; no in-process permanent scheduler or external delivery promise.
- Private read/unread inbox, once-set read acknowledgments, typed internal targets,
  optional preferences and explicit validated IANA display timezone. Verified source
  changes and exact current missing-document facts produce deduplicated notices;
  obsolete or unknown requirement sets do not generate invented missing notices.
- Arabic RTL/English LTR tracker/inbox UI with loading/denied/retry states, safe text,
  independent observation and display timestamps, stable retry intent keys, explicit
  source rebase/submission/deletion-review wording and internal document links.

## Assumptions and external boundaries

- The catalog has no source round ID. Distinct NFC-normalized/case-folded reviewed
  labels identify rounds across date revisions. A sole unlabeled round has one
  stable single-round key; duplicate/ambiguous labels fail closed. No new academic
  year, source round or rename mapping is invented automatically.
- `required_documents` supplies DOCUMENT slots only. Empty means unknown; richer
  mandatory form/action schemas are absent. Ready is bounded checklist readiness,
  not a guarantee of complete/accepted external portal submission.
- The Ready back edge and direct reported outcomes from Submitted follow existing
  documented engineering assumptions. Terminal reversal/official precedence is not
  invented. Official ingestion stays disabled without lawful approval.
- Reminder offsets retain R18 elapsed minutes20160/10080/4320/1440, not guessed
  calendar days across DST. Subtract from the verified donor instant, then display
  in the student's chosen zone. Future due times only; no initial missed-reminder
  catchup or guarantee that a student views a delivered notice. Asia/Hebron is the
  sourced initial display assumption, not an inferred device location.
