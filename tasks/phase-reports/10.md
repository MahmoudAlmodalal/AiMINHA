The executable path is LOCAL, server-fixed synthetic data only. All named real
adapters have no HTTP implementation and deny execution. Keys, approval fixtures,
flags or injected registry entries cannot activate them. Public real egress also
requires reviewed terms and remains closed. No $0 guarantee depends on trusting
an advertised vendor allowance to stop billing.

One gateway request reuses an existing synthetic credit operation and its stable
price/day/global admission. Each physical attempt rechecks the current global
stop without counting that logical operation again. Zero limits or lowered
limits already exceeded stop dispatch. Default admission remains100 operations
per Hebron day; physical windows use independent explicit UTC boundaries.

Models/configs sharing a provider/account/unit share one nonoverlapping quota
window. Fresh, evidenced, free-only measurement and a known worst-case bound are
required before allocation. Cloudflare bookkeeping requires Neurons and at most
8,000 units; synthetic meter fixtures do not attest actual vendor capacity.
Unknown/stale/exhausted capacity stops work. Baselines cannot reset or be replaced
by overlapping windows; no live refresh ingestion is implemented. Unresolved old
window calls block the pool in later windows too.

DISPATCH_INTENT and allocation commit before adapter invocation outside database
transactions; an outer transaction is rejected. Engineering defaults:10-second
timeout,2 attempts per config,3 total, circuit opens after3 failures for30 seconds,
with one serialized half-open probe. Leases also end at credit reservation expiry,
provider-window end or meter freshness cutoff. Only proved retryable
NOT_EXECUTED failures may retry/fall back. Their upper-bound allocation is
conservatively consumed, not claimed as actual measured usage or refunded.
UNKNOWN retains physical capacity and credit holds in RECONCILING and forbids
blind fallback/refund. Intent commit is the revocation boundary; a future live
transport cannot recall bytes already sent. Account, consent, approval and fence
are rechecked before accepted-result commit.

Output is limited to exact quotations of known synthetic evidence, citation IDs
and language, with no tools, eligibility overrides or unsupported claims.
GatewayResult and the strict synthetic financial proof settle atomically.
Persistence failure leaves no debit; the retained intent is recovered through
the timeout marker. SQL supplements trusted service clocks/fences with immutable
evidence, capacity conservation and exact request/attempt/result/credit-state
equivalence. RECONCILING has no unproved exit. Telemetry excludes raw prompts,
responses and exception messages.
