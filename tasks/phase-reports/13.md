# Phase13 — support tickets, AI triage and business-hours SLA

Date: 2026-10-20. Baseline: `a4d96ca` (Phase12 implementation), branch `main`.

**PASS — LOCAL_SUPPORT_TICKET_SLA_AI_TRIAGE_GATE.** Owned tickets carry an
append-only six-state history, the first human response is measured on an
administrator working calendar that excludes weekends, holidays, closed hours and
daylight-saving distortion, an automated answer never satisfies that obligation,
ticket attachments are explicit revocable shares readable only by the currently
assigned agent with an audit trail, and the AI triage works only from the allowed
context with mandatory human handoff and a bounded free allowance that never moves
credits. This is a local gate; provider-backed triage, staffing evidence, external
integrations and release approval remain gated.

## Delivered behavior and bounded coverage

- Owned tickets with category, subject, first message and an explicit
  `request_human` flag. A student reads only their own ticket; another student
  gets 404 for it, indistinguishable from a missing identifier. Commands require
  an active verified-adult session, CSRF, a stable idempotency key and, where the
  aggregate changes, a current quoted revision (compare-and-swap, 412 on stale).
- Exactly the six documented states (`NEW`, `AI_HANDLING`, `AWAITING_ADMIN`,
  `AWAITING_STUDENT`, `RESOLVED`, `CLOSED`) with an append-only event log that
  records the from/to state, the kind, the actor, the reason and the observed and
  recorded instants. SQL guards make events, calendar policies and messages
  immutable, freeze the ticket owner/identity and forbid a backwards revision.
- Business-hours first-response measurement on the administrator working calendar.
  Defaults are the sourced ones: Asia/Hebron, Sunday–Thursday, 09:00–17:00,
  critical 4 / high 8 / normal 24 / low 48 business hours. Business time is
  resolved in the calendar's local wall clock, so weekend, holiday and
  daylight-saving transitions are exact rather than approximated; unit cases
  assert both the local due wall time and the UTC instant across the 2026
  spring-forward and autumn-back transitions.
- The clock starts once per measured cycle (student request, AI handoff or agent
  assignment) and ends at the first substantive administrator message. A short
  administrator note is recorded as an acknowledgement and does not satisfy it; an
  AI message never satisfies it; time spent waiting for the student afterwards
  cannot change a metric that was already met. The due instant, the target minutes
  and the bound calendar version are frozen at the start of the cycle, so a later
  policy or priority change never silently rewrites an in-flight commitment. A
  reopen inside the configured window starts a new cycle.
- Resolution, closure and rating. A student confirmation resolves and closes the
  ticket; otherwise a bounded worker sweep closes a resolved ticket after the
  configured quiet period (7 days). Reopening is allowed for 14 days from closure
  and refused afterwards with `REOPEN_WINDOW_CLOSED`. A rating is recorded once and
  stays editable inside the 7-day window, and is refused outside it.
- Ticket attachments are explicit, revocable, versioned shares of a private asset
  the student already owns. They are not a library permission: the administrator
  queue exposes metadata only, and the thread, the attachment metadata and the
  attachment bytes are readable only by the administrator currently assigned to
  that ticket, with the support scope and a current MFA proof. Every allowed and
  denied access is audited, a revoked share is refused, and reassigning the ticket
  immediately removes the previous agent's access.
- AI triage with a strict allowed context: the current ticket's subject, category
  and messages. It suggests a category and a priority and records the reason; the
  effective category and priority stay administrator-controlled. Handoff to a human
  is mandatory on a student request, on low confidence, after two consecutive
  uncertain answers for that student, on financial topics, on security topics and
  on critical deadline pressure. The AI answer is stored in the thread with an
  explicit author marker, so a student always sees what produced it.
- Bounded experiment: five free AI messages per student per Asia/Hebron business
  day, plus a platform-wide support counter compared against the same global AI
  cap the credits module enforces. The credits global counter is read and never
  written, because the ledger's conservation guard equates it with credit
  operations; support AI therefore cannot be laundered through the financial
  ledger, and an AI reply never debits or reserves a credit. When an allowance is
  exhausted the request is handed to a human with an explicit reason instead of
  pretending to be an AI answer.
- Administrator surface: queue with state, category, priority, assignment, due
  calculation and warning/breach markers and no student identity, subject or
  message content; assignment; replies; transitions; explicit classification with
  a reason; the ticket SLA projection; aggregate support metrics (volumes, first
  human responses, warnings, breaches, resolutions, ratings, average first
  response in business minutes) and the versioned working-calendar policy.
- Arabic RTL / English LTR student support page: ticket list separated into open
  and closed cases, ticket creation, the thread with student/AI/human markers, the
  shared attachment list, the measured first-response expectation, the resolution,
  reopen and rating controls, and loading, denied and unavailable states.

## Assumptions and external boundaries

- **ASSUMPTION:** the working calendar, per-priority targets, quiet period,
  reopening window, rating window and AI allowance live in one versioned, auditable
  policy object whose shipped values are the sourced defaults. They are
  engineering defaults and experimental targets, not a published service level.
- **ASSUMPTION:** provider-backed triage is not implemented in this phase because
  no approved `STUDENT_PRIVATE` route exists (Phase10 outcome). The deterministic
  local classifier is the honest, testable behaviour and is recorded as an
  engineering choice forced by the approval state, not as a model capability.
- The AI context excludes attachment bytes and metadata, other tickets, profile
  data, account state and credit balances by construction; the context builder
  cannot request them. No provider call, prompt persistence or raw log of ticket
  text exists in the module.
- A short administrator reply is an acknowledgement. The 40-character rule is a
  server-side rule so an agent cannot declare a one-word note substantive to avoid
  the measured obligation.
- The platform-wide AI support counter is compared with the credits global cap
  rather than written into the credits counter; a concurrent credit reservation
  and a support AI message can therefore overshoot the shared cap by one
  admission. The cap is an experiment bound, not a financial invariant.
- Staffing evidence behind the 4/8/24/48 business-hour targets, a production
  scheduler, database-role/RLS and load evidence, legal retention and erasure
  review for support data, the administrator console (Phase15), accessibility
  browser interaction and release signoff remain independent.

## Failures found and corrected

- The first focused run failed because the support calendar model field named
  `timezone` shadowed the Django `timezone` module inside the class body. The
  model now imports the helper under an explicit alias; no behaviour changed.
- The AI allowance initially incremented the credits global admitted-operation
  counter. The credits conservation guard refused it, because that counter is
  structurally tied to credit operations. Support usage now has its own platform
  counter compared against the same cap, and a regression test asserts that the
  financial counter is untouched.
- Ticket mutations were not persisted in several service paths, so the measured
  clock, the state and the revision were lost. Every aggregate mutation now
  persists under the row lock, and the event sequence stays contiguous.
- Denial audits written inside the aggregate transaction were rolled back with
  it. The assignment check now runs outside the transaction, so the denial
  survives, and the in-transaction re-check keeps the compare-and-swap safety.
- The worker sweep wrote the state change without persisting the aggregate, so
  an auto-closed ticket stayed resolved; and the sweep advanced the event
  sequence without saving it, producing a duplicate sequence. Both were fixed and
  covered by regression cases.
- The `state` field of the support projection collided with another module's
  enum in the generated schema. Explicit enum overrides and distinct support
  component names resolved it before the contract was regenerated.
- A read-only support path used a row lock outside a transaction and returned
  HTTP 500 in the real server. The account lock is now taken only inside a write
  transaction.
- The real HTTP smoke harness failed three times on harness defects, not product
  defects: the isolated database was created through an environment pointing at
  the not-yet-created database, the administrator fixture lacked the test host
  and the values argument, and the administrator session needed a server-authored
  MFA proof. Each was corrected and the flow then passed.
